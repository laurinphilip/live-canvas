# Code of conduct

Live Canvas aims to be a welcoming project for people of every experience level and background.

Be respectful, patient, and specific. Critique ideas and code, not people. Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private vault content. Assume good intent while accepting corrections when impact differs from intent.

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
